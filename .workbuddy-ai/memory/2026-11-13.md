# 2026-11-13 — the critic was dead, not stale

## Foresight plan, Level 0: the diagnosis changed

`docs/FORESIGHT_PLAN_2026-11-08.md` §1 said the consequence critic was "stale and
half-blind" (trained 2026-09-20, before the goal-vision slots existed). Measured, it is
worse: **the critic is collapsed.**

Over 12,297 real decisions:

| measurement | value |
|---|---|
| first-layer units that never activate | 30 / 32 |
| second-layer units that never activate | 31 / 32 |
| Q range across every state | 0.3115..0.4046 (0.0123 in a 1.30 band) |
| share of Q variance explained by intent alone | 71.5% |
| holdout rank correlation, critic | +0.0595 |
| holdout rank correlation, 10-number intent prior | +0.0905 |

The critic LOSES to a lookup table. Its blend of 0.3 has been applying a near-constant
vector to every decision since 2026-09-20: a fixed tilt toward SHOOT, away from
THROUGH_BALL and CROSS, everywhere on the pitch.

## Root cause (reproduced, not guessed)

The TD two-stage recipe at `lr=1e-2` kills the ReLU stack — Adam steps large enough to
push every unit's pre-activation negative, after which the gradient is zero forever.
Re-running the exact v5 recipe reproduces it (28/32 and 30/32 dead). At `lr=5e-3`:
32/32 alive, holdout rho +0.0713 vs prior -0.0790 (delta +0.1503).
Default changed to 5e-3 in `critic_surrogate.py`.

## The Level 0 gate was vacuous

"Q(SHOOT) above Q(SAFE_PASS) in an unpressured central clear chance" **already passes**
with the dead v5 critic — 100% of 36 real clear chances. It passes because SHOOT has the
highest intent prior, not because the critic sees anything. Replaced by `critic_health()`:

- **Q spread** ≥ a quarter of the reward band (else the advantage term cannot move a decision)
- **beats a table** — holdout rank correlation > intent prior fitted on train only

Dead units are reported but do NOT decide. First draft of the guard failed any critic with
>25% of a layer dark; a synthetic run then hit rho 0.986 with 23/32 second-layer units dead.
Anatomy is a hint, performance is the verdict. Loss cannot catch this either — a dead net
has an ordinary weighted MSE, because predicting the mean IS the optimum once gradients die.
